# 2003 — Lore so far

The story as it stands, and what is still open. Last updated 2026-11-04.

## The frame

The main character is an adult today: 31, in 2026. He is remembering one day in 2003, when he was 8, because something traumatic happened that day. What happened is still to be decided.

The whole game is his memory of that day. Everything we see comes from the kid's imagination and from the way he processes and deals with the trauma, so most of what happens is not real.

Leaning (2026-11-04): it can be told poetically, so the game never has to describe exactly what the trauma was.

The ending twist: the player finds out these are all the memories of a 31-year-old in 2026, looking back at one day in 2003.

## Characters

- **The boy.** 8 years old in 2003. "Lucas" is a placeholder name for now.
- **Mum.** Grounds him and locks him in his room until his homework is done. She should move in a creepy way, like Nikki in the film *Obsession*.
- **Luna.** The family cat, his companion through the game. In the attic she becomes a monster.

## The photo in the corridor

A mystery that needs to be solved. An early-1900s family photo hangs in the corridor: a mother, a father, an 8-year-old son, and Luna. Probably connected with the basement. How it ties into the rest of the lore is still open.

## How it starts

It's 2003. You're 8 years old, coming back from school. Your mother grounds you and locks you in your room until you finish your homework. You open your backpack and start, but the last sentence is missing one word. You have to find its letters around the room by solving environmental puzzles.

When you're done you call your mother, but she doesn't answer. So you kill time playing Zelpa on the TV, and you get sucked inside it. You finish the level by finding the key: once you have all the letters, it opens the door of your room.

## Levels, in order

Every room is a different level, and each one is more uncanny than the last.

1. **Bedroom**

3. **Bathroom**

5. **Basement**
6. **Backyard at night**
7. **Attic**

## Puzzles and ideas so far

**Bedroom**
- Close the blinds and look up to find a letter.
- The posters: combine them to get one letter. The posters in the room now are placeholders.
- The PC: it may give a letter, or it may be where you look up rules and hints. Not decided.
- Zelpa, inside the TV: find the key. Once all the letters are found, it opens the bedroom door.
- The room is still full of placeholder bits and needs a polish pass, the PC included.

**Living room + kitchen** (needs a polish pass)
- At some point a man rings the doorbell. Very creepy: his character must be inspired by *Courage the Cowardly Dog*. What he is there for is still to be decided.
- Something to solve with the clocks.
- On the fridge hangs a paper: a treasure map of the garden, drawn by the kid. You need it in the garden to find things.

**Bathroom**
- A UV torch to find blood stains. It is in the bathroom cabinet.
- Blood all over the bathroom, with hints of what to do there. You only see it with the UV torch.
- A vent or something similar high up, maybe in the ceiling. To reach it you fill the room with water and swim up.

**Backyard**
- Work out what to do with the flamingos.
- Use the kid's treasure map from the fridge to find things around the garden.

**Basement**
- Inspired by the Backrooms.
- Probably connected to the early-1900s photo hanging in the corridor. Luna is in that photo too. This is a mystery still to be defined.
- The basement is set in those years: it throws him back there.

**Attic**
- A spirit radio (spirit box).
- Luna becomes a monster.

## What you can do in the build now

- Bedroom: close the blinds, then look up to find a letter.
- Play Zelpa on the TV and get pulled inside it.
- Bathroom: shut the door once Luna is in, then run the bath until the room is really full (the funniest part so far). The UV torch is in the bathroom cabinet: shine it on the corridor wall.
- Garden: the hose, the swing, the slide, digging in the sand, the pool past the trees.
- Basement: take the torch beside the cellar doors and pull the door three times to get in. Still very raw; it will become something like the Backrooms.

## Already in the house

Details in the build so far that could become clues:

- Every clock in the house reads 6:04 PM, the stopped ones too. (In the build the stopped clocks still show twenty to four; to be changed.)
- In the corridor, writing in blood on the wallpaper next to the photo, pointing at the attic hatch. You can only see it with the UV torch. The "HELL→" in the build is a placeholder test: the real words will be something creepy.
- Mum in the kitchen, arms folded, watching the corridor. Now and then she cuts carrots at the chopping board and looks up at you.
- The front door is locked and goes nowhere. Mum's bedroom door is locked.
