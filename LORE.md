# 2003 — Lore so far

The story as it stands, and what is still open. Last updated 2026-10-31.

## The frame

The main character is an adult today, 31 years old. He is remembering one day in 2003, when he was 8, because something traumatic happened that day. What happened is still to be decided.

The whole game is his memory of that day. It plays a lot with the kid's imagination, so most of what happens is not real.

The ending twist: the player finds out the boy is now 31 and all of this was a memory.

## Characters

- **The boy.** 8 years old in 2003. "Lucas" is a placeholder name for now.
- **Mum.** Grounds him and locks him in his room until his homework is done.
- **Luna.** The family cat, his companion through the game. In the attic she becomes a monster.

## How it starts

It's 2003. You're 8 years old, coming back from school. Your mother grounds you and locks you in your room until you finish your homework. You open your backpack and start, but the last sentence is missing one word. You have to find its letters around the room by solving environmental puzzles.

When you're done you call your mother, but she doesn't answer. So you kill time playing Zelpa on the TV, and you get sucked inside it. You finish the level by finding the key to the door.

## Levels, in order

Every room is a different level, and each one is more uncanny than the last.

1. **Bedroom**
2. **Living room + kitchen**
3. **Bathroom**
4. **Backyard**
5. **Basement**
6. **Backyard at night**
7. **Attic**

## Puzzles and ideas so far

**Bedroom**
- Close the blinds to find a letter.
- Solve something on the PC to get another.
- Connect the posters so they form a letter.
- Zelpa, inside the TV: find the key to the door.

**Living room + kitchen**
- At some point a man rings the doorbell. Very creepy, inspired by *Courage the Cowardly Dog*. What he is there for is still to be decided.
- Something to solve with the clocks.

**Bathroom**
- A UV torch to find blood stains.
- What you have to do is drawn on the walls.
- A vent or something similar high up, maybe in the ceiling. To reach it you fill the room with water and swim up.

**Backyard**
- Work out what to do with the flamingos.

**Basement**
- Inspired by the Backrooms.
- Probably connected to the early-1900s photo hanging in the corridor. Luna is in that photo too. This is a mystery still to be defined.
- The basement is set in those years: it throws him back there.

**Attic**
- A spirit radio (spirit box).
- Luna becomes a monster.

## Already in the house

Details in the build so far that could become clues:

- The stopped clocks show twenty to four. The digital clocks in the kitchen read 6:04.
- In the corridor, "HELL→" written in blood on the wallpaper next to the photo, pointing at the attic hatch. You can only see it with the UV torch.
- Mum in the kitchen, arms folded, watching the corridor. Now and then she cuts carrots at the chopping board and looks up at you.
- Basement: small shoe prints going from the steps to an old sofa, none coming back. A box labelled LUCAS. A child's chair facing the corner. A locked door at the end of a corridor.
- The front door is locked and goes nowhere. Mum's bedroom door is locked.
